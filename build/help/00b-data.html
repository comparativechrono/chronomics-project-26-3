The project data

referencecome back to this often

Everything the project needs is in the read-only folder /data/examples/scn5a/. Each file is a small slice of a large public dataset. This page says what each one is, where it came from, and what to cite.

See the files for yourself: , or open the Files tab.

The reference genome and annotation — reference/

FileWhat it is
GRCh38_chr3_SCN5A.fa.gzA 1.5-Mb slice of human chromosome 3 (GRCh38) around SCN5A, as a FASTA file (with its .fai and .gzi indexes). Only bases 37,900,001–39,400,000 are real; the rest are written as N so the file stays small while positions are still those of the whole chromosome.
genes_GRCh38.gff3The protein-coding genes of the locus (SCN5A, SCN10A and neighbours) with the exons of their MANE Select transcript (Ensembl).
SCN5A_exons.bedThe exons of SCN5A's MANE transcript, numbered from the gene's start.
SCN5A_promoter_variants.bedThe two variants, rs41310749 and rs41310239.
SCN5A_haplotype_block.bedThe extended haplotype block you will define in stage 5 (shown in the genome browser).
dbsnp157_scn5a_2Mb.vcf.gzThe known variants of the 2-Mb locus with their standard names (rsIDs), from dbSNP build 157 (NCBI).

The pangenome — hprc/

hprc_scn5a_promoter.vcf.gzThe Human Pangenome Reference Consortium release 2 graph (Minigraph–Cactus v2.1) across the promoter, as whole-allele "bubbles" — the pangenome's own view of the sequence.
hprc_scn5a_2Mb.vcf.gzThe same pangenome across the 2-Mb locus, decomposed into SNVs, insertions and deletions. 231 people, 462 phased haplotypes.
hprc_samples.tsvWho the 231 people are: population, superpopulation, sex.

1000 Genomes — 1000g/

1kgp_scn5a_2Mb.vcf.gzThe phased variants of the 1000 Genomes Project high-coverage panel across the locus: 3,202 people (2,504 of them unrelated), short-read, already split into one variant per line.
1kgp_samples.tsvWho they are: population, superpopulation, sex, and whether they are in the unrelated set of 2,504.

Biobank-scale data — gwas/ and gtex/

gwas/brugada_Barc2022_SCN5A_SCN10A.tsvThe SCN5A–SCN10A locus of a genome-wide association study of Brugada syndrome (Barc et al. 2022), as GRCh38-harmonised summary statistics: for each variant, the effect on disease risk and its P-value.
gtex/GTEx_v8_SCN5A_rs41310749.tsvThe effect of rs41310749 on how much SCN5A RNA is made in each tissue of GTEx v8 (an eQTL table) — you will also query GTEx live.

Motifs and primates — motifs/ and primates/

motifs/JASPAR2024_CORE_vertebrates_nr_pfms.txtThe binding matrices of transcription factors (JASPAR 2024 CORE, vertebrates) — including the clock proteins (CLOCK, BMAL1, the bHLH family) and cardiac factors (GATA4, TBX5, NKX2-5).
primates/scn5a_promoter_primates.faThe SCN5A promoter of ten primates (human, the apes, macaques, vervet, mouse lemur), from the Ensembl EPO alignment — for the conservation stage.
All public, all anonymous

The human genome data are population reference panels and summary numbers, not patients. There are no names and no medical records. The guide never asks you to use anything else. Never put personal or patient data into this workbench or an AI tool.

What to cite

When you write up, cite the resources you used: the Human Pangenome Reference Consortium (Liao et al. 2023, and the release 2 preprint); the 1000 Genomes Project (2015) and its high-coverage panel (Byrska-Bishop et al. 2022); dbSNP; Ensembl; GRCh38; the Brugada GWAS (Barc et al. 2022); GTEx (2020); JASPAR (2024); and the programs (samtools, bcftools, PLINK 2, kalign, R). The key paper on the two variants is Park et al. 2012. The reading list has these, and the References section of each tool's help page has the programs.
