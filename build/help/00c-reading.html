The reading list

throughoutread a few each stage

You do not need to read all of these at once. Start with the two marked start here, then read into each theme as its stage arrives. Find them in the Databases tab (Europe PMC card) or search their title; write short notes in your logbook as you go.

The two variants and the SCN5A promoter start here

  • Park JK, Martin LJ, Zhang X, Jegga AG, Benson DW (2012). Genetic variants in SCN5A promoter are associated with arrhythmia phenotype severity in patients with heterozygous loss-of-function mutation. Heart Rhythm 9(7):1090–1096. — The paper on rs41310749 and rs41310239. Read it closely.
  • Yang P, Kupershmidt S, Roden DM (2004). Cloning and initial characterization of the human cardiac sodium channel (SCN5A) promoter. — background on the promoter the two variants sit in.
  • van den Boogaard M, et al. (2012). A common genetic variant within SCN10A modulates cardiac SCN5A expression (J Clin Invest). — how DNA near the neighbouring gene controls SCN5A; important for stage 7.

The clock and the heart start here

  • A recent review of the circadian clock in the cardiovascular system (search "circadian clock heart review"). — how the clock shapes heart rate, blood pressure and arrhythmia risk through the day.
  • Jeyaraj D, et al. (2012). Circadian rhythms govern cardiac repolarization and arrhythmogenesis (Nature). — a clock output (KLF15) controlling the heart's electrical recovery.
  • Work from the Chronomic Medicine Group on SCN5A, E-boxes and the clock (ask your supervisor for the group's recent abstracts and papers).

Brugada syndrome and cardiac conduction

  • Barc J, et al. (2022). Genome-wide association analyses identify new Brugada syndrome risk loci (Nature Genetics). — the GWAS you use in stage 10.
  • A review of Brugada syndrome and of SCN5A in inherited arrhythmia (search "Brugada syndrome SCN5A review").

The methods: pangenomes, haplotypes, biobanks

  • Liao WW, et al. (2023). A draft human pangenome reference (Nature). — what a pangenome is and why it matters.
  • The 1000 Genomes Project Consortium (2015). A global reference for human genetic variation (Nature); and Byrska-Bishop M, et al. (2022) for the high-coverage panel you use.
  • A short explainer of linkage disequilibrium and haplotype blocks (search "linkage disequilibrium tutorial").
  • The GTEx Consortium (2020). The GTEx atlas of genetic regulatory effects (Science). — eQTLs, used in stage 10.

Evolution and conservation

  • Prüfer K, et al. on the Altai and Vindija Neanderthal and Denisovan genomes. — the archaic genomes of stage 9.
  • A primer on sequence conservation and what it says about function (search "phyloP GERP conservation").
For your logbook
